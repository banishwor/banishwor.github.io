LeikaiFix

Prototype • Paused

Civic Infrastructure & Public Accountability

A community-driven civic tech platform empowering citizens across Manipur to report local infrastructure issues (potholes, water supply, power grid failures) by uploading photos with automatic geotagging and mapping to the concerned MLA constituency.

Project Status & Takeaway

Included transparency analytics like the "Most Neglected Constituencies" and a public civic accountability leaderboard. While technically viable, active development was paused to safeguard against sensitive regulatory, administrative, and legal risks.

On-Device AI Keyboard

R&D • Archived

Edge NLP & Privacy-Preserving Android IME

An intelligent, privacy-first Android keyboard based on the open-source FlorisBoard. Integrated a lightweight offline open-source language model directly on the handset to paraphrase, correct grammar, and modify writing tone with zero cloud dependencies.

Engineering Insight & Hardware Limits

While the local model performed smoothly on high-tier flagship processors, token inference latency and RAM pressure hindered typing responsiveness on budget Android devices (the primary target audience). Archived pending lighter models and faster mobile NPUs.

FlorisBoard Fork / Edge AI Study
